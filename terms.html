Billow for iPhone · Effective November 5, 2026

Billow helps you track and cancel subscriptions. It is information and tooling, not financial or legal advice.

The service

Detection, reminders, letters and suggestions are generated from the data you provide and public catalog data. Check amounts and dates before acting; merchants, banks and app stores remain the source of truth.

Billow Pro

Pro is sold through Apple as a monthly or yearly auto-renewing subscription or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app for your storefront. Payment is charged to your Apple account at confirmation; the yearly plan may start with a 14-day free trial.

Renewal and cancellation

Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Cancel any time in iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or from Settings › Billow Pro › Manage subscription in the app. Refunds are handled by Apple.

Free tier

Free tracks up to 7 active subscriptions. Beyond that, subscriptions stay visible but become read-only until you upgrade. No data is deleted when a trial or subscription ends.

Mailboxes

Connecting a mailbox is optional. Billow reads receipt emails on your iPhone as described in the Privacy Policy, and you can disconnect at any time.

No fees on savings

Billow never charges a share of what you save and never contacts merchants on your behalf. You send every letter and message yourself.
