01Every detail

See exactly what each one costs you.

Every subscription shows its real cost per day and per year, a price-history chart that flags every increase, and the full billing history.

  • Cost per day, month and year, in your home currency
  • Price history with every increase marked
  • Mark paid, pause, snooze or cancel in one tap
02Payday planner

Plan around the day you get paid.

The calendar shows daily totals, marks your payday and tells you exactly what leaves your account before the next one, with 3, 6 and 12-month forecasts.

  • Month grid with daily totals
  • “Before payday” total that updates as charges land
  • Forecasts for the months ahead
03Insights

See where a year of subscriptions goes.

A 12-month chart shows what you spent, what it costs per day and which months peak. Billow flags forgotten subscriptions and counts what you saved by cancelling.

  • 12-month trend with your cost per day
  • Possibly forgotten and saved-this-year totals
  • Your most expensive subscriptions, ranked
04Trials & price hikes

Never pay for a trial you forgot.

Add a free trial and Billow sets a cancel by deadline a day before it converts, with a Live Activity countdown on your Lock Screen. Price changes and forgotten subscriptions get flagged too.

  • Time Sensitive reminders before a trial converts
  • Alerts when a price goes up
  • “Still using this?” check-ins and duplicate detection
05Add in seconds

Share a receipt. Billow does the rest.

Search 500+ known services, or share a receipt, screenshot, PDF or bank statement. Billow finds the recurring charges on your iPhone and explains why it thinks each one repeats.

  • 500+ services with logos, plans and billing cycles
  • Import from receipts and bank statements, on device
  • Home Screen, Lock Screen and StandBy widgets
06Email receipts

Find subscriptions in your inbox.

Connect Gmail, iCloud Mail or any IMAP mailbox and Billow finds the receipts for your subscriptions. Mail is read on your iPhone with read-only access, only the merchant, amount, date and billing cycle are kept, and nothing is ever uploaded. Disconnect any time. How Billow handles your email

  • Gmail, iCloud Mail and other IMAP mailboxes
  • Only receipt emails from the last 18 months, read-only
  • Message text is discarded right after reading
